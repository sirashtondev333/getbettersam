    Who is Samir and what has happened to him?

    Sam aka devmansam is a member of the software engineering cohort 100devs. He is known for his kindness, his skill, his sense of humor, his eagerness to help others, his iconic hair and sunglasses, and his fondness for pizza. He has built multiple projects dedicated to helping freelancing devs find prospective clients. He was initially diagnosed with hepatitis in April; while the doctors were replacing the stents in his bile and pancreatic ducts to treat it they found a lesion on his pancreas that quickly evolved into stage 3 pancreatic cancer. On June 15th he underwent surgery to remove the head of his pancreas, his gallbladder, part of his bile duct, and his duodenum (the beginning part of the small intestine). On June 22nd he came home from the hospital, and within the next few weeks he will begin six months of aggressive chemo.
