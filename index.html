He has built multiple projects dedicated to helping freelancing devs find prospective clients. was initially diagnosed with hepatitis in April. As of June 15th he underwent surgery to remove remove the head of his pancreas, his gallbladder, part of his bile duct, and his duodenum (the beginning part of the small intestine). On June 22nd he came home from the hospital. Within the next month he will begin six months of aggressive chemo.
